The route

16:30

Elkano 1 Gaztagune

Elkano Kalea 1 · cheese Map ↗

Order thisThe Teyedu, with an Idiazabal to compare.

A cheese shop that looks well past the Basque country for what it stocks. Good place to start while everyone's still sober enough to taste properly.

17:05 2 MIN WALK

Lurka Chocolate

Bengoetxea Kalea 1 · chocolate Map ↗

Order thisA cup of the drinking chocolate.

Mexican bean-to-bar, made here by a Mexican of Basque descent reviving a trade route that once ran between this port and his home country. Buy a tablet to take home — they travel well.

17:40 6 MIN WALK

San Martín Market

Urbieta Kalea 9 · market Map ↗

Look forKokotxas at the fish counters.

Ten minutes, nothing to buy. Kokotxas are cod and hake throats — chefs and grandmothers queue for them in the same line. This is where the food you'll eat tonight comes from.

18:00 4 MIN WALK

Antonio Taberna

Bergara Kalea 3 · tortilla Map ↗

Order thisTortilla de patata and a house vermouth.

The best tortilla in the city, and the reason we're here at six rather than seven. A fresh one comes out around 18:30 and it sells out. I'll queue — you hold standing room at the bar.

We leave at 18:55 sharp

What to order at each stop

Pulled from what people actually keep mentioning in reviews, so you're not reading a Basque menu cold at the counter.

Elkano 1 Gaztagune

★ 4.7 · 185

Ask for the Teyedu. It comes up unprompted again and again — one reviewer was handed a taste and bought it on the spot. Pair it with an Idiazabal to see the contrast, and let the owner talk; that's most of the value here.

Teyedu cheese ↗The shop ↗

Lurka Chocolate

★ 4.8 · 204

The drinking chocolate, and a tablet to take home — several regulars describe being sent back with orders from friends. There are a few seats and good coffee and pastries if we're running early.

Drinking chocolate ↗The shop ↗

San Martín Market

★ 4.3 · 6,138

Nothing to buy. Look at the fish counters — reviewers single out the all-women fishmonger stall and the queue in front of it. That's the thing to watch, not the produce.

Kokotxas ↗The market ↗

Antonio Taberna

★ 4.5 · 2,954

Tortilla de patata with the house vermouth — the fresh batch lands around 18:30 and sells out, and one reviewer suggests being there by 18:15. Beyond that, two things come up repeatedly: the txuleta, and a newer meatball pintxo. Space is tight, so expect to stand at the outside bar.

The tortilla ↗Txuleta ↗The bar ↗

Bouiboui

★ 4.9 · 84

Highest-rated stop of the night. It's a specialty food and natural wine shop rather than a bar — reviewers rave about the grilled cheese, the sauces, the artisan pasta and the crisps. The owner is the draw and hands out recommendations freely. Good place to buy something to carry home.

Grilled cheese ↗The shop ↗

Bodega Klandestina

★ 4.9 · 50

Don't choose. Tell the owner what you've been drinking and let him pick — that's what nearly every review describes, and they all describe it working. Modern Spanish alongside natural wines. This is where to buy bottles for the suitcase rather than at the airport.

The shop ↗

Manojo

★ 4.6 · 250

Navajas — razor clams — are called a must more than once. Also strong: bonito with leche de tigre, the handmade tagliatelle with clams, the gilda and charcuterie, and pork in tomato sauce. Small plates, so three or four between two people. Menu changes with the market. Not fast — plan to sit.

Navajas ↗Bonito, leche de tigre ↗The bar ↗

SUA.MUKA

★ 4.1 · 570

The weakest score of the eight, and worth knowing why: food and location get consistent praise, but service and portion-for-price draw repeated complaints. Tasting menu around €65, wine pairing about €28 more. Go à la carte for a drink at the bar, or skip it — the river-meets-sea terrace is the real reason to bother.

The terrace ↗

Practical

Budget €60–90 each for the whole evening, food and drink included.

Bring cash. Most of these won't split a bill six ways. I'll pay at each stop and we'll settle up at Manojo.

About 3km of walking, all flat, but you're on your feet for five hours. Wear accordingly.

Message me if you're running late — don't try to catch us up at stop four.

Monte Igueldo

Paseo de Igeldo 183 · funfair on a cliff Map ↗

Ride these twoRío Misterioso 小船, then Montaña Suiza 小火車.

The 1912 funfair on the headland at the far west end of the bay — the one in the reel. Everything else up there is skippable. Two rides, about €3 each, and the terrace between them.

Park shuts 14:00–16:00 · we go up at 16:00

The two rides

This is the whole reason we're on the hill. Do them in this order — the boat while the light is still high, the cart after.

Corrected timing. This page used to say 16:05, which was impossible — you cannot be at the bottom of the hill at 16:00 and on a ride at the top five minutes later. The funicular runs every 15 min, takes 3, then it is a walk to the gate. Realistically 16:25. Still plenty: you have until 17:20.

16:25
Río Misterioso 小船

The wooden boat. It runs along the outside of the cliff with the whole bay below you, then ducks into a tunnel and comes out the other side. Two minutes, about €3, and it is the shot everybody sent each other. Sit at the back for the view over the bow.

16:50
Montaña Suiza 小火車

The little blue carts, open-topped, on a track that runs right along the cliff edge. Built 1928 and it feels it. 400m, about 50km/h, ninety seconds, roughly €2.50. Hold onto your phone properly — there is nothing between you and the sea.

17:05
The terrace, and the group photo

Free once you're in. The classic frame is the whole of La Concha with Santa Clara island in the middle. This is the postcard everybody recognises. The Torreón tower costs €3 or €4 extra and you do not need it.

17:20
Down, and back to town

Taxi, not the funicular. The queue going down is the known bottleneck and 18:15 at Antonio is not negotiable. Straight to Bergara Kalea, about ten minutes.

The terrace — the group photo

The postcard: the whole curve of La Concha with Santa Clara island dead centre and the city behind. Stand at the stone balustrade and shoot back at the group with the bay behind them. Best light is late afternoon, which is exactly when we're there. The Torreón — an old wood-fired lighthouse — charges extra for a higher version of the same view. Skip it.

The official view ↗
+
Three rules

Wrist strap or zip pocket on both rides. There is nothing between the track and the sea, and no one is getting a phone back. Bring cash — rides are paid one at a time and cards aren't reliable up there. And the whole park is faded Belle Époque paintwork and hand-painted signs — shoot the peeling blue and gold detail, not just the view. That's the bit that doesn't look like everyone else's San Sebastián photos.

Getting there · 交通

We are aiming for Funicular Plaza, at the bottom of the hill behind Ondarreta beach. Be there by 16:00. If you want the rides earlier, take bus 16 instead of walking and be at the top by 15:45, waiting when the gate reopens.

行路
Walk it — 60 min, free

Leave Manojo at 15:00. West over the Kursaal bridge, along La Concha, past Ondarreta, and you arrive at the funicular square. Flat the whole way, and it walks off the lunch.

Walking route ↗
巴士
Dbus line 16 — about 15 min, ~€2

Board at Boulevard 19 or Avenida de la Libertad, get off at Funikularea — the stop is right in front of the funicular square. Every 15–30 minutes. Do not stay on to Igeldo village at the top; that stop is a kilometre from the gate along a road with no footpath.

Taxi — 6–10 min, ~€15–20 the car

Two cars for six. Worth it if we are behind at Manojo. Ask for el funicular de Igueldo, not the park, unless you want to be driven all the way up.

纜車
Funicular up — 3 min, ~€4.75 return

Running since 1912, oldest in the Basque Country, 320m up the hillside. Every 15 minutes. Buy singles, not returns, since we are taxiing down — and have coins, the ride kiosks up top prefer cash.

Practical

Budget roughly €60–90 each — a rough estimate, not a quote. Manojo is the one real meal and the biggest single line.

Bring cash. Most of these won't split a bill six ways. I'll pay at each stop and we'll settle up at Manojo.

Walking: roughly 2km in the morning, and about the same again in the evening. The walk out to the funicular is 4.5km and takes an hour, flat the whole way — bus 16 or a taxi if you'd rather not.

Budget for the hill: under €70 for all six. Funicular about €4.75 return or roughly half that one way, and rides are pay-as-you-go from about €2.30 — the two we want are around €3 each. Bring cash. Plus two taxis down.

Book Manojo. Saturday lunch for six isn't a walk-in.

11 Oct only · optional switch

Arcangues festival

Optional · easier with a car

Herri Uzta · Mutxikoaren Eguna

Sunday 11 October 2026 · 11:00–20:00 · free. Théâtre de la Nature, Bourg d'Arcangues. The published event description is centred on Basque traditional dance, music and language; the detailed 2026 programme is still to come.

Transport reality

Car / taxi is much better than public transport if you want to insert Arcangues between Saint‑Jean‑de‑Luz and Biarritz. It avoids turning the afternoon into multiple local-bus changes.

But do not assume easy parking. Arcangues normally has free village parking near the centre, including parking close to the village roundabout. However, the festival takes place at the Théâtre de la Nature in the same village-centre area and festival-specific parking / overflow arrangements for 11 Oct have not yet been published. Expect the normal village spaces to be under more pressure than on an ordinary Sunday.

Best practical approach if you choose it:
Saint‑Jean‑de‑Luz lunch → taxi / hired car to Arcangues → ask to be dropped near the village centre / Théâtre de la Nature → festival ~60–90 min → taxi onward to Biarritz.

If self-driving: aim to arrive earlier rather than exactly at a headline performance time. Use the official village parking first, but be prepared to park farther away and walk. Check the final festival notice shortly before 11 Oct for any temporary parking, traffic or shuttle instructions.

If you stay no-car: this remains an optional detour, not part of the base itinerary. I would only do it if the final programme has something your group really wants to see and you are comfortable using taxis for both Saint‑Jean‑de‑Luz → Arcangues and Arcangues → Biarritz.

😄 Friends moment: this is the most “local Basque culture” option of the day — watch one dance/music session rather than trying to see the whole festival. The value is joining the atmosphere, not staying for hours.

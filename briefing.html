What to bring

The bits people forget

Feet
Proper walking shoes.Six days have a hike. Chamonix Wednesday has scrambling.
Cold
Layers, gloves, sunglasses.Valley mild, 3,842m well below freezing. Glacier glare is severe.
Power
Power bank.Cold flattens phones at altitude. Essential on 4 and 6 Oct.
Money
Francs, then euros.Switzerland isn't in the euro. Cash for buses and small bars.
Water
A bottle.Fountains everywhere. Buying at 2,000m is its own kind of expensive.
Phone
Tickets in the wallet app before you fly.No signal inside mountains.

How we do it

Three rules, that's all

Eating
One bite, one drink, standing. Then move.The failure mode is sitting down at stop three and never leaving.
Walking
Say it early, not late.Every walk has a bail-out. Nobody has to finish anything.
Weather
Calls get made the night before.Never over breakfast. If something moves, I'll say so.

The one to remember

3,842m

Aiguille du Midi, Tuesday 6 October. Higher than Jungfraujoch, reached in twenty minutes by cable car with no acclimatising.

Walk slowly, drink water, and say something early if your head goes. It passes, but it's better managed at the start than at the top.

Chamonix
Tue 6 Oct

Aiguille du Midi · 3,842m

Highest point of the whole trip.

see text
🇫🇷 TUE 6 OCT · AIGUILLE DU MIDI · 3,842m
HIGHEST POINT OF THE TRIP — higher than Jungfraujoch

07:45 At the cable car station
08:10 First car up (nothing runs earlier in October)
      Last round trip down: 15:00

At the top, in this order:
 1. Lower terrace — DON'T cross the bridge yet.
    Climb the stairs to the upper terrace (points 16-17).
    Best viewpoint and almost everyone walks past it.
 2. The bridge
 3. Espace Vertical
 4. Terrace 3842 — STEP INTO THE VOID
    (not guaranteed — closes for wind, frost, crowds)
 5. Espace Mont Blanc
 6. Hypoxia level
 7. Le Tube
 8. Open-air terrace

⚠️ 3,842m in 20 minutes with no acclimatising.
Slow, water, gloves. Say something early.

Cloudy? We swap days 6 and 7.

11:00 · Casco Viejo

Old Town + pintxos

One-click Old Town food route ↗

~11:15

1 · Bar Bacaicoa

Small, old-school stop in Plaza Miguel de Unamuno. Their own menu is unusually clear about the house specialties.

Top tasty food

Champiñón a la Gloria · Chorizo al Infierno · Croqueta de bacalao. If six of you want to sample efficiently, the official menu also lists a combo.

😄 Fun moment: order the “Chorizo al Infierno” for whoever claims to tolerate spice best.
Google Maps ↗ Official food examples ↗

~11:50

2 · La Olla de la Plaza Nueva

A modern pintxo bar on Plaza Nueva with a large rotating counter plus hot pintxos made to order.

Top tasty food

From its current official pintxo list: taco de chuletón, Alcachofa XL, taco de atún rojo, bacalao al pil-pil. For the most photogenic bite, look for the tomato-shaped cream-cheese pintxo. Availability can change by season.

📸 Instagram moment: photograph the colourful pintxo bar first, then one “tomato” pintxo against the Plaza Nueva arcades.
Google Maps ↗ Official pintxo photos ↗

~12:25

3 · Gure Toki

Plaza Nueva institution mixing traditional Basque flavours with more modern miniature cooking.

Top tasty food

Bilbao's official pintxo guide highlights oxtail croquette, scallop with potato and breadcrumbs, grilled foie with apple and Pedro Ximénez, and cod with pil-pil as the most demanded pintxos.

📸 Instagram moment: choose the most sculptural pintxo on the counter and shoot it with the Plaza Nueva arches behind.
Google Maps ↗ Official pintxo gallery ↗

~13:00

4 · Sorginzulo

Another Plaza Nueva stop with a strong competition record. You do not need to eat at all four bars — treat this as the final optional stop.

Top tasty food

The restaurant's own site records a 2026 first prize for its cod pintxo and a 2026 second prize for its Pintxo Burger. It also notes its tortilla reached the national tortilla competition in 2025. Ask what award pintxo is actually available that day.

😄 Fun moment: each person picks one pintxo purely by appearance; compare who chose best after one bite.
Google Maps ↗ Official examples / awards ↗

Food rule

Do not force all four. Bacaicoa is the fixed first stop; then pick two of La Olla / Gure Toki / Sorginzulo depending on the counter and appetite.

~14:15 → 15:00

Walk toward the museum

Leave Casco Viejo and follow the river toward Abandoibarra. The point is the change from the medieval Old Town to modern Bilbao before you arrive at Gehry's building.

📸 Zubizuri moment: cross Calatrava's white footbridge and shoot back along its curved arch.
Zubizuri · Google Maps ↗ Photo examples ↗

~15:00 → 17:30

Guggenheim Museum Bilbao

The museum is officially open on Tuesday from 10:00 to 19:00. Two to two-and-a-half hours is a sensible visit for this itinerary.

The official essentials include Richard Serra's The Matter of Time, Jeff Koons' Puppy and Louise Bourgeois' Maman.

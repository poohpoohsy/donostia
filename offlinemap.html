Free · install before we fly

Three apps
to download.

Google Maps offline gives you driving directions only — no walking, no transit, no trails. These three cover what it can't, and all work with no signal.

In order of how much you'll use them

Get these on your phone

Ten minutes on wifi. Download the maps inside each app, not on the mountain.

Organic Mapsfree · no account

The important one. Fully offline and it routes you along footpaths — the thing Google won't do. Download Switzerland, France and Spain inside the app. Alpine trails, hut names and waymarks all show.

organicmaps.app →

SwitzerlandMobilityfree

The official Swiss hiking network — every yellow signpost and route number on the real Swisstopo map. Best way to check a trail is open and how long it takes.

schweizmobil.ch →

SBB Mobilefree

Swiss trains, buses and mountain lifts minute by minute, and it holds our tickets. Needs signal to look up a connection — so screenshot the day's plan the night before.

sbb.ch →

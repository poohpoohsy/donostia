How to use these

Three things worth knowing

One-tap directions. Every stop has a Map ↗ button. It opens that exact place in Google Maps — the actual pin, not a name search. Tap it and walk.

Whole-day routes. Each page has a route button near the top that chains the entire day together, in order, on foot. Open it before you set out and it stays loaded when signal drops.

Live weather. The three readings above are real, right now. On the mountain days the forecast fills in by itself from about five days out and colours each day green, amber or red at summit height — which is a different thing from valley weather, and the reason most people get it wrong.

Opening hours checked against current listings. Mountain plans move with the weather — anything that changes gets said the night before, not over breakfast.

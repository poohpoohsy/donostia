Dinner

Do not book one.

20:30 · hunger decides

After a 14:30 Casa Cámara seafood lunch, I would not reserve another restaurant. You may genuinely not want dinner.

If hungry, walk into the Old Town and have one or two pintxos only. My simple choice is Bar Txepetxa: one anchovy pintxo, then stop. It is open Friday evening and keeps the first night fun without competing with tomorrow's crawl.

If nobody is hungry: tea / yoghurt / fruit near the hotel and bed. That is a successful dinner plan too.

One decision at 20:30: hungry = one/two pintxos; not hungry = stay in. No second big meal and no late night. Saturday is the proper San Sebastián food day.
